Admissions 2026–27

Admissions are open for Class 8, Class 9 and Class 11 in all groups. Here is everything you need to apply.

How admission works

Most families finish the whole process within a week.

  1. Send an inquiry

    Fill in the form below or visit the admissions office.

  2. Get a call back

    Our office contacts you within one working day to fix a test date.

  3. Entry test

    A short test in English, Mathematics and Science for placement.

  4. Counselling

    A 15-minute meeting with parents to confirm the right group.

  5. Confirm your seat

    Submit documents and pay the admission fee to reserve the seat.

Students joining Class 10 or 12 mid-course are considered case by case.

Documents required

  • Copy of the student's B-Form or CNIC
  • Copy of the father's or guardian's CNIC
  • Previous class result card (Matric result for Class 11)
  • School leaving certificate (original)
  • Character certificate from the previous institution
  • Four recent passport-size photographs
  • Migration certificate, if coming from another board

Students with 90% or more in their last exam receive 50% off tuition; 80% or more receive 25% off.

Class 8: the foundation year

Class 8 is where we build the habits and basics a student needs for Matric science. Teaching is concept-first: students learn why a formula works before they memorise it, and they write every day in English and Urdu.

Subjects

SubjectPeriods per week
English6
Urdu5
Mathematics6
General Science5
Islamiat3
Social Studies3
Computer2

How we teach Class 8

  • Short weekly quizzes in Mathematics and Science
  • A reading hour every week in the library
  • Monthly report card shared with parents

At a glance

  • EligibilityPassed Class 7
  • Class timings8:00 AM – 1:30 PM
  • Admission feeRs. 5,000
  • Monthly feeRs. 3,500

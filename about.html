About STARs College

A Mirpur college for Classes 8 to 12, built around one idea: every student can shine with the right teaching and steady attention.

Our story

STARs College began in 2011 as a small coaching centre for Matric science students in Mirpur. Parents asked us to teach their children full time, and within four years we had grown into a full college with Intermediate groups in Pre-Medical, Pre-Engineering, ICS and I.Com.

Today more than 1,200 students study with us from Class 8 to Class 12. We are affiliated with the BISE Mirpur, and our students go on to medical colleges, engineering universities and business schools across Pakistan.

What has not changed is the way we teach: small classes, weekly tests, and teachers who know each student's strengths and gaps.

Our vision

To be the college in Azad Kashmir that parents trust most to prepare their children for university and for life.

Our mission

To give every student strong concepts, disciplined study habits and honest guidance, through qualified teachers, a regular test system and close contact with parents.

Our values

  • ExcellenceWe set high standards and help every student reach them.
  • DisciplinePunctuality, attendance and respect are part of daily life on campus.
  • IntegrityHonest assessment, honest reports to parents, honest advice about careers.
  • CareEach student is known by name, and no one is left behind in a crowded class.

Campus facilities

Everything a Class 8 to 12 student needs, on one campus.

Physics lab

Full set of apparatus for every Matric and FSc practical in the board syllabus.

Chemistry lab

Fume-safe workbenches and a supervised chemical store for titrations and salt analysis.

Biology lab

Microscopes, specimens and models for dissection and slide work.

Computer lab

Networked computers with programming tools for ICS and Matric Computer students.

Library

Textbooks, reference guides, past papers and a quiet study hall open after classes.

Classrooms and transport

Bright classrooms with multimedia, and pick-and-drop routes across Mirpur city.
